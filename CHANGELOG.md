## [1.0.0] - Unreleased

### Added
- Physically simulated falling leaves: trees shed leaves that flutter and glide down, pile up on the ground and fade
  away after a few minutes.
- Leaves react to walking and sprinting players and mobs, landings, projectiles, wind charges and explosions;
  sneaking leaves them alone.
- Leaves float and drift on water and burn in lava; broken or decaying leaves blocks release a burst of leaves.
- Gusty wind that grows in rain and thunderstorms.
- Leaf colors match the tree and biome, with optional autumn colors; different shapes for broad-leaved trees,
  birch, spruce and cherry.
- Rustling sound when walking through leaves.
- Client config for the number of leaves, fall rate, wind and interaction strength.
